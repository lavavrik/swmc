Diesel — how to wire the vehicle

Diesel + 8-speed. Engine clutch (mandatory) then a T: starter motor on one pipe, transmission on the other. Shift on engine RPS (up 12, down 7), not wheel speed — that is why the old car stuck in 3rd.

pipe — RPS / torque pipe — fluid electric on/off number composite

Start, launch, shift

Engine clutch is always on with ignition. Ignition on, RPS < 3: starter clutch locks, motor cranks, drive clutch stays 0. HUD shows START.

After start: starter clutch locks, motor follows the pedal in every gear and stays on through shifts. Regen clutch: slight slip on the pedal (`Regen idle` 0.15), locks on lift-off or brake. HUD: RPS + speed km/h, small gear, B/T/M/S bars.

Gearboxes

GB1 toward engine off 1:1 on 6:5. GB2 toward off 1:1 on 3:2. GB3 toward wheels off 3:1 on 1:1 (reduction is OFF, 1st = ×0.33). Optional reverse box on Reverse out (−1:1).

Need electric on the boxes to switch. Pipes carry RPS; fuel is a separate fluid pipe into the engine.
